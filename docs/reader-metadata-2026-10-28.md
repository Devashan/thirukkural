# Complete reader metadata — 28 October 2026

Development snapshot `2026.10.28.1` completes the reader-facing fields that
were still visibly absent after the 27 October meaning revision:

- Kurals 37, 184, 223, 268, 329, 354 and 388 now have commentary-backed,
  AI-assisted English drafts. Their existing `flagged` status, uncertainty
  notes and `needs_tamil_reviewer` flags remain unchanged.
- Every chapter has a sourced English heading.
- Every chapter is assigned to one of ten sourced iyal divisions, with Tamil
  and English iyal names, paal placement, order and a division source.

## Structural source

The chapter headings and division boundaries come from V. R. Ramachandra
Dikshitar's *Thirukkural English Translation* (1949), in the Project Madurai
electronic text:

https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0665.html

That edition explicitly lists these chapter ranges: 1–4, 5–24, 25–37, 38,
39–63, 64–73, 74–95, 96–108, 109–115 and 116–133. The source identity, credit
and locator are stored with every chapter rather than implied by the app.

## Seven completed drafts

The editorial choices use the traditional commentaries and English versions
collected on the corresponding `thirukkural.io/kural/{number}` pages. The
drafts choose the following readings while keeping the prior warnings visible:

- **37:** the bearer and rider illustrate virtue's fruit.
- **184:** harsh speech to someone's face does not excuse harmful backbiting.
- **223:** generosity acts before a person must voice their need.
- **268:** self-mastery brings the reverence of other living beings.
- **329:** killing is described as degrading work without introducing a caste
  label into the modern explanation.
- **354:** even mastery of the five senses is fruitless without true insight.
- **388:** `இறை` is rendered “lord” in the kingship context, not capitalised
  “God”.

These remain development explanations, not qualified Tamil-language
approvals. A future review may revise any of the seven without changing the
Tamil source text.
