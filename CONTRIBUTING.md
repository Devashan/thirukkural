# Contributing

Thank you for helping make the Thirukkural easier to access and study. This is a small, reviewed project: public issues and pull requests are the contribution path for now.

## Report or propose a change

1. Identify the Kural by chapter number and position (or its derived global number), or name the affected paal/iyal/chapter.
2. State what you see, what you propose and why. Link an exact source page, edition/page number or a reproducible transliteration example. For Tamil corrections, distinguish a source transcription issue from a different reading in another edition.
3. For a pull request, change only the relevant data, rules or documentation; include a fixture or comparison when a transliteration rule changes.
4. Wait for maintainer review before a correction enters a versioned release. Additional Tamil-language review may be required for textual readings and pronunciation spellings.

Please do not paste copyrighted modern translations or commentaries, personal information, or large third-party data dumps. Contact the maintainer first if you want to contribute an original English explanation or another substantial work: author credit, review and permission for a clear open licence must be agreed before publication. Do not submit text written by someone else without evidence of permission.

Generated romanisation is rebuilt from the source and shared rules. Report a bad output with input and expected reading; avoid editing a single generated verse without an underlying rule or documented exception that can be reviewed.

Contributions should be your own work or material you are permitted to offer for open reuse. By submitting a contribution you affirm that you have the rights needed to submit it and agree to its publication under the rights terms explicitly stated for that contribution and confirmed before merge. **A proposed project-wide CC0 approach for project-owned data is not yet a final licence for historical texts or future explanations.**

For now the maintainer approves releases. A community review group or committee may be introduced when participation warrants it.
